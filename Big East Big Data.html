Where the numbers come from

Final 2023–24 cumulative stats (including postseason) from each school's official athletics website: Butler, Creighton, DePaul, Georgetown, Marquette, Providence, Seton Hall, St. John's, UConn, Villanova and Xavier. The mid-February snapshot used on the Trends page and player "stretch run" cards comes from a hand-entered spreadsheet of each team's top rotation players.

Qualifying rules

  • All leaderboards: played in at least 75% of his team's games (minimum 3)
  • FG% and TS%: 3+ field-goal attempts per team game, at least 10 total
  • 3PT%: 1.5+ three-point attempts per team game, at least 6 total
  • FT%: 1.5+ free-throw attempts per team game, at least 6 total
  • Usage rate: 10+ minutes per game
  • Minimums rise automatically as teams play more games

Data checks

Every player's points match his made shots, and every team's player rows add up to its official totals (team rebounds and turnovers aside). Four players in the February snapshot have numbers that don't add up; their stretch-run cards say so. Possessions use the standard estimate: FGA − offensive rebounds + turnovers + 0.475 × FTA.
