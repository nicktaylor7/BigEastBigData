Where the numbers come from

Final cumulative season stats from 2018–19 through 2025–26 (including postseason) from each school's official athletics website: Butler, Creighton, DePaul, Georgetown, Marquette, Providence, Seton Hall, St. John's, UConn, Villanova and Xavier.

Qualifying rules

  • All leaderboards: played in at least 75% of his team's games (minimum 3)
  • FG% and TS%: 3+ field-goal attempts per team game, at least 10 total
  • 3PT%: 1.5+ three-point attempts per team game, at least 6 total
  • FT%: 1.5+ free-throw attempts per team game, at least 6 total
  • Usage rate: 10+ minutes per game
  • Minimums rise automatically as teams play more games

Data checks

Every player's points match his made shots in all eight seasons, and every team's player rows add up to its official totals (team rebounds and turnovers aside). The one exception is on a school's own page: Seton Hall's 2018–19 opponent totals are off by 2 points, and we show them as published. Possessions use the standard estimate: FGA − offensive rebounds + turnovers + 0.475 × FTA.
